**Zhenghua Primary School Open House 2026**

We warmly invite prospective parents and families to our Open House on Thursday, 2 July 2026, from 2.30 pm to 4.30 pm at the School Hall.

Come and experience what makes Zhenghua Primary a nurturing place for your child to learn and grow. The afternoon will feature an address by our Principal, lively performances by our Performing Arts Co-Curricular Activities (CCAs), and opportunities to view CCA booths and interact with our student leaders and teachers.

Register your interest by scanning the QR code above. We look forward to welcoming you!
