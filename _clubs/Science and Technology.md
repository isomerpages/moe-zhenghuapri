# Science and Technology

The Science and Technology Club gives students the opportunity to explore the world of science and technology in fun and meaningful ways while building important skills for today’s digital and AI-driven world. It aims to spark curiosity, nurture creativity, and encourage students to ask questions, investigate ideas, and discover how science and technology are connected to everyday life. Through these experiences, students will develop 21st-century skills such as critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving, while discovering the possibilities of our technology-driven world.

Students can look forward to:

* Hands-on activities with digital and scientific tools.
* Creating digital content.
* Coding to solve real-world problems.
* Science challenges and experiments.
* Participating in exciting science and technology events and competitions.
* Learning journeys.
